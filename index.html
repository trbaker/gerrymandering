Glossary

Electoral district
An area whose voters elect one representative. Every U.S. state is divided into House districts with roughly equal populations.
Redistricting
Redrawing district boundaries inside a state so each district has about the same number of people. It happens after every 10-year census, once seats have been reapportioned (re-divided) among the states.
Gerrymandering
Drawing district lines to give one party or group an advantage.
Packing
Cramming the other side's voters into one district they win by a landslide, so their extra votes are wasted.
Cracking
Splitting the other side's voters across several districts so they lose all of them narrowly.
Shape score
How tidy a district's shape is (the Polsby–Popper score). 1.0 is a perfect circle; near 0 is stringy or spread out.
